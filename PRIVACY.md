# PUSH TOTAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

PUSH TOTAL is designed to work without an account or server.

## Data we collect
PUSH TOTAL does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics data.

## Motion sensor data
When you use Pocket Count, PUSH TOTAL accesses iPhone motion sensor data to estimate repetitive push-up movement. Motion samples are processed in real time on the device. Raw motion samples are not uploaded to a server and are not retained after the counting session.

## Push-up records
Push-up totals and individual set records are stored locally on your iPhone using on-device storage. They remain on the device unless you delete the app, delete the records inside PUSH TOTAL, or choose to export/share them yourself.

## Third parties
The current version does not include advertising, analytics, account, or third-party data collection services.

## Retention and deletion
Local push-up records remain until you delete them. You can delete individual records or all app records from the Record screen. Because PUSH TOTAL does not operate a user account or server database, there is no remote account data to delete.

## Permissions
Motion & Fitness permission is requested only when you choose Pocket Count. If you decline, manual push-up logging remains available.

## Changes
If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact
For privacy questions, use the developer contact information shown on the PUSH TOTAL App Store product page.
