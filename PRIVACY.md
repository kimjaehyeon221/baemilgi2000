# PUSH TOTAL Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

PUSH TOTAL is designed to work without an account or server.

## Data we collect
PUSH TOTAL does **not** collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The current version does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or analytics data.

## Motion sensor data
When you start Pocket Count, PUSH TOTAL accesses iPhone motion sensor data to estimate repetitive push-up movement. Motion samples are processed in real time on the device while the counting session is active. Raw motion samples are not uploaded to a server and are not retained after the session.

## Push-up records
Confirmed push-up totals and individual set records are stored locally on your iPhone using on-device storage. They remain on the device unless you delete records inside PUSH TOTAL, reset app records, remove the app, or explicitly export/share records yourself.

## Third parties
The current version does not include advertising, analytics, user-account, cloud-sync, or third-party data-collection services.

## Retention and deletion
Local push-up records remain until you delete them. You can delete individual saved records or reset all app records from within PUSH TOTAL. Because PUSH TOTAL does not operate a user account or server database, there is no remote account data to delete.

## Permissions
Motion & Fitness permission is requested when you choose Pocket Count. If permission is declined, Pocket Count cannot measure a set until the permission is granted. The app does not provide unrestricted manual push-up entry.

## Export
If you choose the record export feature, iOS presents the system share sheet. PUSH TOTAL does not automatically upload the exported text; the destination is selected by you through iOS.

## Changes
If a future version adds cloud sync, analytics, advertising, accounts, or other data processing, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact
For privacy questions, use the developer contact method provided on the PUSH TOTAL support page or App Store product page.
