## English

Your push-up pedometer

### Promotional text

### Description
**Let push-ups add up like steps.**

PUSH TOTAL is a lightweight counter built around one exercise and one meaningful number: your lifetime push-ups.

Tap Pocket Count to start a 10-second setup. Use the first 5 seconds to place your iPhone fully in a front pants pocket, then hold your starting position for the remaining 5 seconds. After the start sound and haptic, on-device motion sensing estimates repetitive push-up movement.

When repetitive movement stops for 5 seconds after your set, PUSH TOTAL automatically ends the session and shows a confirmation screen. Before saving, you can adjust the detected count within ±10.

Features
- Pocket Count using iPhone motion sensors
- 5-second pocket placement + 5-second position setup
- Automatic finish after 5 seconds of no repetitive movement
- ±10 pre-save correction
- Lifetime and daily push-up totals
- Week, month, and seven-day history
- 100 PUSHES = 1 BRICK wall visualization
- Adjustable Pocket Count sensitivity
- Delete records or reset all local records
- Record export
- Local storage with no account required

PUSH TOTAL does not use the camera. Raw motion samples are processed on-device and are not uploaded or retained on a server.

Pocket Count is an estimate and can vary with pocket placement, cadence, device position, and form. If needed, adjust the count before saving or change Pocket Count sensitivity.

## App Review notes
PUSH TOTAL uses iPhone motion sensor samples only during a user-started Pocket Count session. The reviewer can test it by tapping Pocket Count, allowing Motion & Fitness access, placing the iPhone in a front pants pocket during the first 5 seconds, holding a push-up start position during the next 5 seconds, and beginning after the start sound/haptic. After the final repetition, remaining still for 5 seconds ends the session and opens a confirmation screen. The estimated count can be adjusted only within ±10 before saving. Raw motion samples are processed live on-device and are not uploaded or retained. If Motion & Fitness permission is declined, Pocket Count is unavailable; there is no unrestricted manual logging mode.
