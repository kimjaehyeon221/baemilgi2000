# App Review Notes — PUSH TOTAL

PUSH TOTAL is a focused push-up tracking utility built around a single primary interaction: Pocket Count.

## How to test Pocket Count
1. Open PUSH TOTAL and complete onboarding.
2. Tap `POCKET COUNT` on the home screen.
3. Allow Motion & Fitness access when iOS requests it.
4. During the first 5 seconds, place the iPhone fully in a front pants pocket.
5. During the next 5 seconds, get into and hold a push-up starting position.
6. Begin push-ups after the start sound and strong haptic.
7. After the final repetition, remain still. Five seconds without repetitive movement automatically ends the session.
8. The app shows the estimated count before saving. The count can be corrected only within ±10, then added to the lifetime total.

Pocket Count uses iPhone motion sensor samples only while a user-started session is active. Motion samples are processed live on-device and are not uploaded to or retained on a server.

The app does not provide unrestricted manual push-up entry. If Motion & Fitness permission is declined, Pocket Count cannot run until permission is granted.

PUSH TOTAL also includes local daily, weekly, monthly and seven-day history, a lifetime total, and a wall visualization where every 100 confirmed push-ups creates one brick. Individual saved records can be deleted and the user can export their records through the iOS share sheet.

No account is required. The current version does not collect health records, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising identifiers, or analytics data. Saved exercise records remain on-device unless the user explicitly exports them.

The product does not make medical, calorie-burn, or injury-prevention claims.
