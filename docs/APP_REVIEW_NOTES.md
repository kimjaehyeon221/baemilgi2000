# App Review Notes — PUSH TOTAL

PUSH TOTAL is a focused personal push-up logging utility. Users can add completed push-ups in a few seconds, while the app maintains lifetime, daily, weekly and monthly totals.

The app includes local history, a seven-day activity view, milestone progress, undo, existing-total import during onboarding, and user-initiated CSV-format export through the iOS share sheet.

No account is required. The app does not collect health records, location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising identifiers. Exercise entries remain on-device unless the user explicitly chooses Export.

The product intentionally does not use streak pressure, social leaderboards, calorie estimates or medical claims.
